What's New?

This format adds three new ways to build a game plan: Glitch Jutsu that wait for the right reaction window, Missions that reward you for pursuing a goal, and Modules that protect the Agent they are attached to.

Glitch Jutsu

Glitch Jutsu are prepared like other Jutsu, but they do not resolve as soon as their Signs are finished. They become Armed Jutsu instead: public, ready-to-use reactions that wait for the right moment.

  1. Prepare the Glitch Jutsu beneath Ninjutsu and pay its listed non-Sign costs. Pay these costs only once.
  2. Form its Signs in order by playing the cards that make those Signs.
  3. When the Sign sequence is complete, move the Glitch Jutsu to the **Armed Jutsu** zone instead of resolving it. A Glitch Jutsu with no Signs becomes Armed immediately after it is prepared.
  4. When its reaction condition occurs, resolve the Armed Jutsu. It does not pay its printed costs again, and then it goes to your Discard.

An Armed Jutsu is public, does not occupy a battlefield position, and does not form Signs. It is waiting for a reaction window, not taking effect yet.

Missions

You may control only one Mission at a time. An attempt to add or play a Mission fails if another copy of that Mission is already in your hand or on your battlefield, or if you have already completed that Mission this battle. A Mission can only be played once per battle.

At the start of your turn, after you draw, each Mission you control advances its Goal by 1 up to its printed Goal. The Mission then checks whether its requirement is met. If it is, resolve the Mission's Complete instruction, then delete the Mission. Missions leave the battlefield after completing, so their rewards are usually the part you want to build around.

Modules

Modules are Daemon subtypes that attach to a card when they enter play. They stay on the battlefield and provide their effect while the attachment matters. When a Module's own effect deletes it, it goes to Discard. If its attached Agent is deleted, the Module is deleted as well.
